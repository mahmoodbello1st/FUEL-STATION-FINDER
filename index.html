About This Project

The Problem

Drivers often spend valuable time driving between stations to find fuel and compare prices, with no shared source of current, nearby pricing.

Target Users

Everyday drivers, commercial transport operators, and logistics riders who need to plan fuel stops around price and distance.

The Solution

Fuel Station Finder combines a live map, a searchable list, filters, and community-reported prices so drivers can decide where to fuel up before they set off.
